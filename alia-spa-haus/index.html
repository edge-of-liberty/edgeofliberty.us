Alia Spa Haus

Handcrafted soaps, bath, and body products inspired by every season — find Alia Spa Haus at The Edge of Liberty Craft Fair in Valparaiso, IN (Northwest Indiana).

Alia Spa Haus is a handcrafted bath and body brand based in NWI, creating small-batch soaps. sugar scrubs, mineral bath salts, body lotions, body oils, hand sanitizers, shower streamers, bath bombs and seasonal collections. Alia Spa Haus was inspired with the belief that everyone deserves a little luxury in their everyday routine. Each product is thoughtfully crafted using quality ingredients and carefully selected fragrances that celebrate the beauty of every season. From relaxing spa-inspired collections to bright and cheerful summer favorites. Whether you're unwinding after a long day, celebrating a season, or searching for the perfect handmade gift, Alia Spa Haus is here to make everyday self-care feel a little more special.
