Hi! We’re B & C 3D Prints, and we love creating unique items that combine creativity, technology, and craftsmanship. Using 3D printing and resin art techniques, we make a wide variety of products including articulated dragons, collectible figures, custom signs, bottle openers, organizers, fidget toys, home décor, and one-of-a-kind gifts.

Many of our resin pieces feature vibrant colors, metallic accents, pressed flowers, and other creative elements, while our 3D-printed designs range from fun and whimsical to practical and personalized. We’re always experimenting with new ideas and designs, so there’s usually something new on our table each time we visit.

Whether you’re looking for a gift, a conversation piece, something functional, or just something that makes you smile, we hope you’ll stop by and see what we’ve been creating. We enjoy sharing our work and helping customers find something special to take home.
