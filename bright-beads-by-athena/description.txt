Bright Beads by Athena is all about color, creativity, and making things that are a little different! I create handmade jewelry with plenty of personality, including fun statement earrings, beaded designs, necklaces, and pendants in styles ranging from cute and colorful to quirky, seasonal, and downright unexpected.

I love combining different beads, charms, shapes, colors, and themes to create pieces you won't find just anywhere. From coffee cups, cupcakes, mushrooms, and dinosaurs to cowboy boots, flowers, holiday designs, and colorful beads, there's something for just about every personality and sense of humor.

You'll also find decorated beaded pens, colorful hanging beadwork, and other handmade creations at my booth. Whether you're shopping for yourself, searching for a unique handmade gift, or just need a pair of earrings with tiny dinosaurs on them, I hope you'll find something that makes you smile.
