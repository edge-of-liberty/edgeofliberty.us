Crazy Kin Creations

Handmade home décor, woodcrafts, art & one-of-a-kind creations — find Crazy Kin Creations at The Edge of Liberty Craft Fair in Valparaiso, IN (Northwest Indiana).

Crazy Kin Creations is a multi-family team of makers who clearly like to keep things interesting! Their booth is packed with handmade home décor, woodcrafts, signs, artwork, seasonal pieces, tumblers, gifts, and plenty of one-of-a-kind finds. With an ever-changing mix of creations and styles, this is one of those booths you’ll want to slow down and explore—you never know what you’ll find.
