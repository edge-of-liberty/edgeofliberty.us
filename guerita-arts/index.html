Guerita Arts

Handmade crochet plushies, bags, accessories & colorful creations — find Guerita Arts at The Edge of Liberty Craft Fair in Valparaiso, IN (Northwest Indiana).

I love turning yarn into colorful, creative pieces that are as fun as they are useful. I make a variety of handmade crochet creations, including adorable plushies and amigurumi, bags and totes, hats, accessories, wall hangings and plenty of other one-of-a-kind pieces.

My creations range from cute animals and whimsical mushrooms to flowers, bold patterns and bright, colorful designs, with something for kids and grown-ups alike. Whether you’re looking for a unique handmade gift, a new bag, something cozy, or simply something that makes you smile, stop by Guerita Arts and see what I’ve been creating!

This vendor currently sells in person at our craft fairs only.
