Padeffed Art Studio MMXVIi

Original art, offbeat apparel & wonderfully unexpected creations — find Padeffed Art Studio MMXVIi at The Edge of Liberty Craft Fair in Valparaiso, IN (Northwest Indiana).

Padeffed Art Studio MMXVII is the wonderfully unpredictable creative world of Valparaiso artist Frank De Nicolo. His work ranges from original art and prints to wall clocks, apparel, hats, and whatever else happens to become a canvas.

Inspired by poetry, storytelling, everyday observations, and a distinctly unconventional point of view, Frank’s work is difficult to put neatly into a category—which is exactly the point.

You never quite know what he’ll bring to the fair. Come see what showed up this time.
