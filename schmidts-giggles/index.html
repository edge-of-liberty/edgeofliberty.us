Schmidts & Giggles

Handmade crochet plushies & custom creations — find Schmidts & Giggles at The Edge of Liberty Craft Fair in Valparaiso, IN (Northwest Indiana).

Welcome to Schmidts & Giggles! We create handmade crochet plushies, custom amigurumi, baby loveys, weighted stuffed animals, and one-of-a-kind fiber art designed to bring smiles to every age. Every piece is crocheted by hand with soft, high-quality yarn and plenty of attention to detail, making each creation as unique as the person who takes it home.

Our collection includes adorable woodland animals, bunnies, ducks, dragons, fantasy creatures, character-inspired plushies, baby comfort loveys, weighted plush friends, and seasonal designs. Whether you’re shopping for a baby shower, birthday, holiday, nursery décor, or simply looking for a cuddly new companion, you’ll find handmade gifts created with care and imagination.

We also love bringing custom ideas to life. If you’ve been searching for a special crochet plushie, personalized gift, or a unique character made just for you, we’d be happy to help create something truly one of a kind.

Schmidts & Giggles is built on the belief that handmade gifts create lasting memories. We enjoy meeting customers in person, sharing our work, and watching people find the plushie that makes them smile. Stop by our booth to browse our latest handmade crochet creations and discover why every stitch is made with love.
