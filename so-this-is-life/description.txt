Heather of SO…THIS IS LIFE creates hand-sewn accessories and home comforts that bring a little personality to everyday life. Her collection includes colorful scrunchies and headbands, wristlet keychains, corn packs, boo-boo packs, pillows, and pillow covers.

From florals and playful prints to classic patterns, her fabric selections offer plenty of ways to show your style. Pick out a scrunchie or headband to finish an outfit, a wristlet keychain to keep your keys close, or a pillow to add a fresh touch to your favorite cozy spot. Corn packs and smaller boo-boo packs round out the collection with a little everyday comfort.

Whether you’re choosing a practical accessory for yourself or a thoughtful gift, stop by Heather’s booth to explore her hand-sewn creations.
