So This Is Life

Hand-sewn scrunchies, headbands, keychains, corn packs, and pillows — find So This Is Life at The Edge of Liberty Craft Fair in Valparaiso, IN (Northwest Indiana).

Heather & Mom of So…This Is Life create hand-sewn accessories and home comforts that bring color and personality to everyday life. Their collection includes scrunchies, headbands, wristlet keychains, corn packs, boo-boo packs, pillows, and pillow covers.

With cheerful florals, playful prints, and classic patterns, there’s something for a variety of styles. Choose an accessory to brighten your outfit, a wristlet to keep your keys close, or a pillow to freshen up your favorite cozy spot.

Whether you’re treating yourself or looking for a thoughtful gift, stop by their booth to meet Heather & Mom and explore their hand-sewn creations.
