The Crafty Gramma

Farmhouse décor, funny signs & handmade gifts — find The Crafty Gramma at The Edge of Liberty Craft Fair in Valparaiso, IN (Northwest Indiana).

Welcome to The Crafty Gramma! Specializing in handmade farmhouse décor, rustic signs, and one-of-a-kind home accents, The Crafty Gramma creates pieces that add personality, warmth, and plenty of smiles to your home. From humorous bathroom signs and farmhouse chicken décor to inspirational plaques, floral artwork, seasonal decorations, and vintage-inspired accents, there’s something for every decorating style.

Every item is thoughtfully handcrafted and designed to make a house feel more like home. Whether you’re decorating your kitchen, bathroom, entryway, living room, porch, or office, you’ll find unique conversation pieces, meaningful gifts, and charming décor that celebrate country living, family, faith, flowers, and the simple joys of everyday life.

Browse a wide selection of decorative wood signs, decoupage art blocks, framed artwork, tiered tray décor, decorative risers, floral accents, mason jar décor, bookmarks, magnets, and seasonal gift items. Whether you’re shopping for yourself or searching for the perfect birthday, housewarming, hostess, holiday, or just-because gift, The Crafty Gramma offers handcrafted pieces you’ll be proud to display and excited to give.

Stop by the booth to discover fresh designs throughout the season—you never know what new farmhouse favorite or laugh-out-loud sign you’ll find next!

This vendor currently sells in person at our craft fairs only.
